Thank you for testing Slowspun.

Google requires twelve testers over 14 days before Slowspun can be released. Here is everything you need.

The short version

  1. Install from the link below, on your phone.
  2. Use the Google account you sent me.
  3. Stay opted in for all 14 days.
  4. Email feedback to the address further down.

Android only

This test runs on Android, and the phone has to be signed in to the Google account you sent me.

An install on any other account does not count toward the twelve testers.

Your tester link

Open the tester link

Tap this on your phone. It only works for the Google account you sent me.

Start here

  1. Tap the link above on your phone, press “Become a tester,” then install from Google Play. Searching the Play Store will not work — it has to come through the link.
  2. Check that your phone is on the account you sent me. The install only counts on that account, and phones are often signed in to a different one from the address people use for email.

Most important

Stay opted in for all 14 days. Google counts twelve testers on the list across 14 unbroken days. An interruption starts the count again from zero.

This is fine

  • Using the app often, or rarely
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Not opening it for a few days

This removes you from the list

  • Signing out of that Google account
  • Using a new phone on another account
  • Pressing “Leave the test” on the link

What to test, and when

A suggested order. Adjust it to fit your own knitting.

When & what
Days 1–2

Open it and look around. Anything that crashes or looks wrong is easier to fix in the first week.

Days 2–4

Regauge a pattern against your own swatch, open a PDF, and size it to your measurements.

Days 4–14

Use the row reader on a project you are actually knitting. Put the phone down mid-row and check the next day that it still holds your place.

Updates install automatically. Nothing for you to do.

Sending feedback

Two things are worth reporting: what took longer than it should have, and where you had to stop and think. Small details are the most useful — a control in an unexpected place, or a step that was not clear.

Email feedback

Or write to slowspun.app+feedback [at] gmail.com — email rather than texting, since I have to summarize all of this for Google at the end.

Common questions

Does it cost anything?
No. Paid features are unlocked for you. Tell me if a real charge appears.
I cannot find the app.
Check your phone is on the account you sent me. If the link errors, tell me — the release may still be in review.
New phone mid-test?
Let me know — the same Google account should carry over.
Where does my data go?
Nowhere. No account, no sign-up, nothing leaves your phone, ever.
I found a bug.
Send it, even if it seems minor. Bugs found now never reach users.
Can I tell people?
Hold off until the app is public — there is nothing to download yet.
When does this end?
14 days after the last tester joins. I will let you know.
